# Advanced Underpowered-Sample Results: Strict BOS-Re-Test, 4H

**Status:** analysis-only; no paper trading or live execution was enabled.
**Protocol:** frozen strict BOS-retest rules; no threshold tuning; aggressor-flow filter disabled; no new indicators.
**Dataset:** official Binance USDⓈ-M Futures 4H archives, using the earliest verified history available per symbol through `2026-08-17 23:59:59 UTC`. BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT begin in 2020; SOLUSDT begins when its verified futures history becomes available in September 2020. The requested August 18–19 tail was not available from the authoritative archive and was not fabricated.

## Executive verdict

> **NO VALIDATED EDGE FOUND.**

The longer sample increased ETH's final untouched-OOS count from 18 to 36 and SOL's from 11 to 22. ETH remains non-negative at the point estimate, but fails the frozen win-rate and bootstrap lower-bound gates. SOL remains positive but underpowered. BTC is near flat but fails the uncertainty, win-rate, and profit-factor gates. The advanced pooled diagnostics are descriptive only and cannot override the asset-level validation protocol.

## Advanced pooled diagnostic

To quantify whether the three assets show a family-level pattern without turning pooling into a new executable strategy, the final-OOS trade ledgers were reconstructed and analyzed using an empirical-Bayes normal-normal partial-pooling diagnostic. This was registered as **descriptive only** and was not used to alter entries, exits, thresholds, sizing, or deployment decisions.

| Asset | OOS trades | Raw mean R | Partial-pooled mean R |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| BTCUSDT | 24 | +0.0366 | +0.2506 |
| ETHUSDT | 36 | +0.4529 | +0.4546 |
| SOLUSDT | 22 | +0.9654 | +0.6840 |
| **Pooled diagnostic** | **82** | — | **inverse-variance mean +0.4569R** |

The within-asset bootstrap P05 of the pooled mean was `+0.1759R`. Leave-one-asset-out pooled means were `+0.6473R` excluding BTC, `+0.4808R` excluding ETH, and `+0.2864R` excluding SOL. These figures are not validation evidence for an asset-specific strategy: the observations are generated by the same rule family, the number of independent asset clusters is only three, and pooling can mask asset-level gate failures. In particular, excluding ETH leaves only 46 trades, and excluding SOL leaves 60 trades dominated by BTC/ETH results that still fail their own gates.

## Decision

The advanced method increased the information available for assessment, but it did not create validated evidence. The correct conclusion is not to select the pooled estimate as a winner and not to paper trade ETH or SOL. The strict BOS hypothesis remains closed for deployment purposes.

The rejected aggressor-flow filter remains frozen and was not rescued. Funding/OI, liquidation, and prospective order-book data remain research/context variables. No additional indicators or new entry rules were introduced.
